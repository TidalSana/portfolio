Photo reconstruction update

The reference photos show the USB at the rear (negative Z), with WKL blockers
at the opposite/front edge. The full-width upper shell sits over a lower body
whose short sides retreat along approximately the rear three quarters, returning
to wide front shoulders through eased doglegs. This produces a real open shelf.

This version models that silhouette, the two-piece seam, a plain inset brass
weight, eight underside screw wells, four empty shallow circular foot pockets,
and a larger USB pill counterbore with a smaller recessed opening. Invented
signature marks, decorative cuts and protruding rubber feet have been removed.
The exposed plate mode hides illustrative switches and PCB.

All dimensions are photo estimates, not measured manufacturer CAD. The original
community keycaps and their existing layout have not been changed. The weight
pocket and foot pockets have separate non-intersecting contours for triangulation.

The recessed side wall remains straight. The experimental outward draft and
concave blend were removed at the user’s correction. The shoulder outline
and upper bevel are unchanged.

Shoulder-only revision: outward slope and small base blend are restricted to
the rounded return (Z 1.15–2.86), fading out at its ends. The long recessed
wall and exterior front wall retain their vertical profiles.

Shell/seam revision from the latest upside-down photo: top case remains the
full-width key-opening shell (Y .06 to .96). Bottom case begins at Y .038;
its side ledge is at Y -.382, leaving a .42 band above the seam when inverted.
Underside datum is now -1.25 with the existing 7-degree slope. Weight, wells
and recesses follow that underside. The shoulder-only slope is preserved.
These remain visual estimates from the photograph.

Ledge width revision: side inset reduced from 1.55 to 1.00 model units
(about 35% narrower). Both straight recess walls move outward .55; rear
wall width follows. Shoulder draft scales to the narrower ledge. Seam,
case heights, overall footprint, and weight remain unchanged.

Screw revision: all eight counterbores have shallow visible metal cross-heads.
Screw axes align with assembly Y (no 7-degree local tilt); hidden shafts cross
the shell seam and terminate inside the top-case height.

Deeper screw revision: heads moved .20 model units farther inside all eight
unchanged .205-radius openings. Shafts remain vertical. Ledge geometry has
matching passages; rear-wall passages use matching color/shadow shader cuts
so the wall does not obscure recessed heads.

Screw seating datum: rear head faces now sit .04 model units above the
ledge plane in the inverted view. Front heads recess at least .30 units.
Counterbore mouths retain their size and position; bores extend to the seats.
